Outpost Zero
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Run a base on the Moon or Mars.

You are the mission commander. Keep four astronauts safe for 8 days by balancing power, air, water, food, radiation shielding and crew spirit. Every choice has a trade-off, just like in real missions.

1. Read the briefingEach morning something happens: a solar flare, a leak, a dust storm. Pick how to respond.
2. Plan the daySpend power blocks on machines and 4 crew shifts on jobs. The little arrows show what tomorrow looks like.
3. Survive 8 daysKeep every system above zero, earn science points and answer Mission Control questions for bonus points.

NASA facts & credits

Outpost Zero turns real space-habitat trade-offs into a game. Every event shows a short fact from real NASA missions and research, and each game day stands for several real days.

Real NASA ideas in the game

  • Artemis and the Moon's south pole: water ice in dark craters (LCROSS found water there in 2009), and the long lunar night of about 14 Earth days.
  • International Space Station life support: water recycling around 98 percent, and astronauts repairing their own equipment.
  • Orion storm shelters and radiation: why water and soil make good shields against solar particle events.
  • Mars missions: the 2018 dust storm and Opportunity, perchlorates found by Phoenix, MOXIE making oxygen on Perseverance, and radio delays of about 4 to 24 minutes.
  • Crew health: exercise on the ISS and NASA's CHAPEA practice Mars habitat.
  • Apollo: about 382 kg of Moon rocks and sticky, sharp moon dust.
